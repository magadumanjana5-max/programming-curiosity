# The Programming World

## Curiosity questions

1. What exactly is programming?
   **Answer:** Programming is expressing instructions and rules in a language a computer can execute. People program to automate work, transform information, and create tools; the computer follows those instructions through its hardware and software.
2. Why was programming invented?
   **Answer:** Programming grew from the need to make machines perform repeatable calculations and operations reliably. It lets people encode a procedure once and have a computer carry it out quickly, consistently, and at a scale that would be impractical by hand.
3. Why can’t humans directly communicate with a computer?
   **Answer:** A computer's processor operates on precisely encoded states, not everyday human meaning. Programming languages provide a more readable layer, and compilers, interpreters, and operating systems translate that layer into operations the hardware can perform.
